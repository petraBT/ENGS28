Activity 13.2.9. Predict, then measure the pulse.

(a)

Before you capture anything, think about three things: the time base that shows one full 20 ms period on the screen; the trigger you will use; and the pwm_value your program will print with the knob at one end, in the middle, and at the other end.
🔗
🔗

(b)

Build, run, and open CoolTerm. Turn the knob from one end to the other: pot_value should run from 0 to 4095, and pwm_value between the numbers you predicted.
🔗
🔗

(c)

On the oscilloscope, trigger on the rising edge and measure the pulse width and the period at each end of the knob, and verify that the pulse width sweeps smoothly from 1 ms to 2 ms as you rotate the potentiometer. Check what you measure against the prediction you made. The two captures below show what a good capture looks like: note their time base and their trigger setting.
🔗
🔗

(d)

Find the knob position that gives a 1.5 ms pulse. Remove and reattach the knob, if necessary, so that it is at the midway position. Then show your sweeping pulse to an instructor.
